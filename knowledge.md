## MIST 2090 course overview
MIST 2090: Introduction to Information Systems in Business teaches students how information technology enables change in organizations. The course covers business value, business processes, data, artificial intelligence, project management, cybersecurity, data visualization, interface design, and digital innovation. Students gain hands-on experience with business technologies and learn how technology can be used to solve organizational challenges. The course combines conceptual material with practical work using Microsoft Excel and other business tools. 【1-843027】

## MIST 2090 grading breakdown
Grades in MIST 2090 are based on several categories. AI and Project Exercises are worth 5%, Class Participation through Top Hat is worth 15%, the Project Portfolio is worth 10%, LinkedIn Learning assignments are worth 5%, eLC Check-Ins are worth 10%, three exams together are worth 30%, and the Final Exam is worth 25%. Students earn an A with 92.5% or higher and an A- with 89.5% to 92.49%. The course uses a traditional plus/minus grading scale. 【1-843027】

## MIST 2090 exams and final exam
The course includes three exams and one cumulative final exam. Exams cover both conceptual course content and Microsoft Excel skills. Excel content is cumulative because later topics build on earlier material. The final exam is fully cumulative and covers material from the entire semester. Students are expected to take exams in person unless alternate arrangements have been approved by the instructor. Missed exams with valid excuses must be completed within five working days after the excused absence ends. 【1-843027】

## MIST 2090 Microsoft Excel requirements
Students must use the desktop version of Microsoft Office 365 ProPlus. The browser-based version is not sufficient for course assignments. Both Windows and Mac versions are supported, although instruction is primarily demonstrated in Windows. Chromebooks cannot be used to complete required coursework. The course also includes a LinkedIn Learning Excel assignment to help students develop spreadsheet and data management skills. 【1-843027】

## MIST 2090 class participation and Top Hat
MIST 2090 uses Top Hat to track class participation. Students participate in lecture activities using either the Top Hat mobile app or a computer. Participation points are awarded based on completion rather than correctness. Students should remain present for the entire class session because attendance codes alone do not provide full participation credit. UGA provides access to the platform at no additional cost. 【1-843027】

## MIST 2090 AI policy
Students may use ChatGPT and other generative AI tools on assignments and projects to support learning. However, students are expected to critically evaluate and improve AI-generated content instead of copying it directly. Generative AI tools are not permitted during exams, quizzes, or the final exam. The course also includes AI exercises focused on prompting, machine learning, AI-assisted Excel workflows, and verification of AI outputs. 【1-843027】

## MIST 2090 late work policy
Unexcused late submissions receive a 20% penalty per day. Late assignments are accepted for up to five days after the due date in applicable categories such as project exercises, project portfolios, and LinkedIn Learning assignments. eLC Check-Ins, exams, and the final exam cannot be submitted late without approved accommodations or excused absences. Students are responsible for monitoring deadlines and course announcements. 【1-843027】

## MIST 2090 project portfolio
The Project Portfolio serves as the major project for the first half of the course. Students create a business plan and design document for a mobile app startup. The portfolio integrates components completed during the semester, including business model canvases, business process models, project management documents, and user experience wireframes. Students may collaborate with others, but each student must submit an individual final project document to receive credit. 【1-843027】
